# 7.6 — Logo, favicon, and splash

## Context

The concept is good and should be kept: a document with text rules,
overlapping an anvil, with a hammer mid-strike. It says "CVForge"
without a word of explanation, it is ownable, and it beats the
folder-and-tick that most CV tools use.

The current file is not usable as a logo, and while investigating where
it would be installed, three unrelated defects turned up in the web
assets — one of them a shipped bug. They are bundled here because they
are the same files and the same pass.

This step is independent of 7.1–7.5. It touches no Dart.

---

## What is wrong with the file

Source: `export-2026-08-19 083422.svg`, 43,434 bytes, `viewBox="0 0 873
1028"`.

| # | Problem | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **It is a bitmap trace, not drawn vector** | Header reads `<!-- Generator: visioncortex VTracer -->`. Path data runs to eight decimal places (`117.66107392`). Edges that should be dead straight visibly wobble. |
| 2 | **Strokes were traced into filled outlines** | There is no stroke to adjust, so line weight cannot be tuned per size — which is exactly what a logo needs at 24px versus 512px. |
| 3 | **Too complex to shrink** | Three overlapping objects plus three document rules. At 24px in the nav rail, or in a favicon, they merge into a smudge. |
| 4 | **The bounding box is unusable** | 873×1028 with the hammer handle escaping top-right. Every slot it needs to fill — favicon, app icon, maskable icon, social card — is square or wider than tall. |
| 5 | **One colour, no brand** | Pure black on a white background attribute. Nothing ties it to `kcPrimaryColor`, and it has no dark-surface variant. |
| 6 | **The silhouettes merge ambiguously** | The document's lower-left disappears behind the anvil, so at a glance it reads as one blobby shape rather than paper-on-anvil. |

---

## Decisions locked

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keep the concept, **redraw** rather than clean up | Simplifying traced paths still leaves traced paths. The geometry here is straight lines, arcs and one taper — faster to draw correctly than to repair. |
| 2 | Draw on a grid with a **single consistent stroke weight** | Fixes problems 1, 2 and 6 at once, and makes an optical-size variant possible. |
| 3 | Ship a **two-mark set**, not one asset | A detailed mark for splash/marketing and a simplified one for 24px and below. One asset cannot serve both, which is problem 3. |
| 4 | **The small mark drops the hammer** | An anvil made of paper is a stronger and simpler idea than three objects competing for 24 pixels. Two shapes survive the size; three do not. |
| 5 | Square the composition to a 1:1 artboard with padding | Every consumer slot is square. Fixing this in the source beats per-export cropping. |
| 6 | Monochrome-capable, with an accent variant | The mark must work as a single flat colour (favicon, maskable icon, print) and have a brand variant for the splash. Not the reverse. |
| 7 | **Do not** put the logo in the nav rail | The rail is 3 destinations of 24px icons; a brand mark competing with them adds nothing. Splash, favicon, app icon, and the CVs empty state are the slots that want it. |

## Deliverables

Source artboard, 1:1, drawn to a 24px-divisible grid:

| Asset | Size | From | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `web/favicon.png` | 32×32 | small mark | currently **20×20**, a non-standard size |
| `web/main-icon.png` | 192×192 | detail mark | splash; currently 179×179, also non-standard |
| `web/icons/Icon-192.png` | 192×192 | detail mark | |
| `web/icons/Icon-512.png` | 512×512 | detail mark | |
| `web/icons/Icon-maskable-192.png` | 192×192 | small mark | must survive a circular crop — keep the mark inside the safe zone, roughly the central 80% |
| `web/icons/Icon-maskable-512.png` | 512×512 | small mark | same |

Keep the redrawn `.svg` sources in the repo (`assets/brand/`, or
`web/` alongside the PNGs) — they are the thing that gets re-exported
next time, and losing them is how the traced file happened.

The maskable icons are the ones most often got wrong: they are cropped
to a circle by Android, so a mark drawn to the artboard edge loses its
corners. That is another reason the small, squarer mark belongs there.

## The three defects found alongside

### 1. `web/manifest.json` is invalid JSON — shipped

```json
"description": ""CV Forge - resume/CV builder web app""
```

Doubled quotes. Confirmed with a parser:

```
INVALID JSON: Expecting ',' delimiter: line 8 column 22 (char 193)
```

A browser that fails to parse the manifest ignores the whole file —
name, icons, theme colour, install behaviour, all of it. Every PWA
affordance the manifest exists to provide is currently off. This is the
highest-value line in this document and it is a one-character-class fix.

While in the file, three Flutter-template leftovers:

- `"name"` / `"short_name"` are `cv_forge` — the package name, not the
  product name. Should be `CVForge`.
- `"background_color"` / `"theme_color"` are `#0175C2`, Flutter's demo
  blue. Should be the app's own ground and accent — after 7.1 lands,
  `kcSurfaceSunken` and `kcPrimaryColor`.
- `"orientation": "portrait-primary"` on a desktop-first CV editor with
  a three-column Studio layout. Should be omitted, or `any`.

These are independent of the logo and can ship first.

### 2. The splash logo is off-centre

`web/index.html`, in the inline styles:

```css
img {
  width: 100px;
  height: 100px;
  position: absolute;
}
```

`position: absolute` with no `top`/`left` takes the image out of
`.main-content`'s flex centring and leaves it at its static-position
origin — so the spinner is centred and the logo is not. Visible on every
cold start, and it is what the review saw at roughly 40% across the
viewport instead of 50%.

Fix by centring it explicitly, or by removing `position: absolute` and
overlaying with a grid. Also note the selector is a bare `img`, which
will style any other image added to this page later.

### 3. The splash spinner is Flutter's demo gradient

```css
background: linear-gradient(#f07e6e, #84cdfa, #5ad1cd);
```

Coral, sky blue, teal — nothing to do with the product. The first thing
a user sees on every load is another framework's default. Restyle to the
brand, or replace the spinner with the mark itself, which is the more
interesting option now that there will be a mark worth showing.

---

## Steps

1. Fix `manifest.json`'s JSON error and the three leftovers. Ship this
   alone if the logo work stalls — it is unblocked and currently broken.
2. Fix the splash centring and gradient.
3. Redraw the detail mark on a 1:1 artboard.
4. Derive the small mark from it (drop the hammer, thicken to survive
   24px, verify inside a maskable safe zone).
5. Export the six PNGs at the sizes above; commit the SVG sources.
6. Point `index.html` and `manifest.json` at them.

## Verification

No Dart changes, so no `flutter test`. Still run the format and analyze
pass if `index.html` or `manifest.json` changed nothing else:

```bash
dart format --output=none --set-exit-if-changed .
flutter analyze
```

**Manifest** — validate before committing, since nothing in CI does:

```bash
python -c "import json;json.load(open('web/manifest.json'));print('ok')"
```

Worth considering a CI step for this. `ci.yml` runs format, analyze and
test; a malformed `manifest.json` passes all three, which is how this
shipped.

**Browser** — hard-reload with cache disabled and confirm: the splash
mark is centred; the spinner is on-brand; the favicon renders in the tab
at 16px and is still recognisable; DevTools → Application → Manifest
parses without error and shows the right name, colours and all four
icons.

**Small-size check** — the one that actually decides whether the redraw
worked. View the small mark at 16, 20, 24 and 32px side by side. If the
anvil and the document are not both still readable at 16px, simplify
further; that is the size the favicon has to survive.

**Maskable check** — DevTools' manifest panel previews the maskable crop,
or overlay a circle at 80% of the artboard. Corners cut off means the
mark is drawn too large.

## Risks

1. **Redrawing loses the character of the trace.** The wobble is a
   defect, but the mark's proportions came from somewhere and a
   too-geometric redraw can read as generic. Keep the original as a
   reference layer.
2. **The two marks drifting.** Two assets means two things to update.
   Derive the small one *from* the detail one in the same source file
   rather than drawing it separately.
3. **Changing the favicon mid-development.** Browsers cache favicons
   aggressively; a stale icon during verification is a cache artefact,
   not a failed export. Hard-reload, and check in a private window
   before concluding anything.

## Not in scope

A wordmark or logotype, a full brand palette beyond what 7.1 establishes,
and any marketing surface. This is the app's own icon set and its splash.
